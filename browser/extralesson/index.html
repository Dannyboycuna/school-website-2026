Taekwondo

Martial Arts

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art that focuses on head-height kicks, jumping and spinning kicks, and fast kicking techniques. Our program develops discipline, confidence, and physical fitness.

Schedule:Monday & Tuesday, 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM
Age Group:All ages
Instructor:Master Mr. DEIXA, Lazaro Manuel Coach and Instructor
Benefits:Self-defense, discipline, fitness, confidence
♟️

Chess

Strategy & Logic

Chess develops critical thinking, strategic planning, and problem-solving skills. Our program caters to beginners and advanced players, with opportunities to compete in inter-school tournaments.

Schedule:Monday & Tuesday, 1:40 PM - 2:30 PM
Age Group:All Ages
Instructor:Mr. DEIXA, Lazaro Manuel Coach
Benefits:Strategic thinking, concentration, memory
🏃

Physical Education

Sports & Fitness

Physical Education promotes healthy living through sports, games, and fitness activities. Students develop teamwork, sportsmanship, and physical skills through various activities including football, basketball, athletics, and more.

Schedule:Wednesday - Thursday, 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM
Age Group:All ages (divided by level)
Instructor:Coach Mr. DEIXA, Lazaro Manuel, Certified PE Teacher
Benefits:Fitness, teamwork, coordination, health
💃

Dance

Creative Expression

Dance classes offer students the opportunity to express themselves through movement. We teach various styles including contemporary, hip-hop, traditional African dance, and ballet, helping students develop rhythm, coordination, and creativity.

Schedule:Wednesday & Thursday, 3:00 PM - 4:30 PM
Age Group:5 - 16 years
Instructor:Ms. Maria, Professional Dancer
Benefits:Creativity, fitness, coordination, confidence
